Association among classes (has-A/is-A relation = > Aggregation, composition ,etc....
*******************************
When we  model classes , We identify relationship between classes

1)Inheritance :  "is a" Relationship
		 ex : Student "is a" Person  , Dog " is a" Animal
		Their will be generic thing and specific thing

2)Association : Structural(Their is no Meaning) Relationship
	i)Composition = "has -A" relationship - whole/part
			ex : lib "has a" books, Person "has a" address				

	ii)Aggregation = "has -A" relationship - strong whole/part  => Nested Classes
			 ex: Computer "has a " Proccessor"
			     Vehicle "has a " engine

3)Realization : among different components
		classes and interfaces/abstract classes
		class implements interface
		ex: someone taking the contract and someone is fulfilling
